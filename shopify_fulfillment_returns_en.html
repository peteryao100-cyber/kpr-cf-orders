Cancellation & Interception

Choose where the parcel is in the shipping lifecycle.

Control: Cancellation requested ≠ cancellation completed. Keep explicit statuses such as requested, hold pending, intercept pending, cancelled and intercept failed. Refund according to approved policy and actual fulfillment outcome.

Business rules requiring owner sign-off

  1. Which system is authoritative for inventory?
  2. What exact payment state permits reservation and warehouse release?
  3. When is physical stock decremented?
  4. Who approves refunds, and what are the exceptions?
  5. Does CT receive returns only, or also fulfill orders?
  6. What is the size exchange and replacement policy?
  7. How are worn safety shoes handled?
  8. Will the ERP later support Amazon Canada and other channels?

Original Chinese Notes / 来源要求

Directly sourced warehouse requirements, separate from the expanded recommendations elsewhere.

信息规范

商品、SKU、条码、规格统一。

一、仓储系统
  1. 独立的shopify本地仓储系统
  2. 实物库存
  3. 成交订单已占用库存
  4. 可售库存
  5. 待质检退货
  6. 破损/冻结库存
  7. 在途库存
  8. 安全库存
  9. 未发货前——客户取消订单，发货时能做到及时拦截
  10. 发货途中——客户取消订单，后台能提醒及时拦截货件
  11. 发货系统——货物发出后，快递单号及时同步到shopify后台
  12. 退款系统——退货订单CT签收后，及时同步到shopify后台
二、下单并确认付款

→ ERP 预占库存 → Shopify 可售库存立即减少 → 仓库发货 → ERP 实物库存正式扣减，同时释放预占。

三、退货入仓流程

客户申请退货 → 创建 RMA/退货单 → 此时不增加库存 → 仓库收到退货 → 核对 SKU、数量和订单 → 质检 → 可再次销售：加入实物库存 → 破损或待处理：进入冻结库存 → ERP 重新计算可售库存 → 同步 Shopify → 按政策执行退款。

Scope note: The original notes define the warehouse foundations. Carrier integrations, fraud holds, API idempotency, staff roles, exchange logic, dashboards, and detailed status mappings are additional recommendations, not stated requirements in the original document.
