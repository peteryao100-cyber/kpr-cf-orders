1. 原则 Principles

① 从"伤害"出发，而不是从"位置"出发 先定义什么数据敏感、为什么敏感（泄露后对谁造成什么伤害），再决定数据放在哪里（本地服务器 / 云端）。Ryan 方案先做"本地 vs 云"的拆分，本文建议顺序倒过来。 Start from harm, not location. Define WHAT is sensitive and WHY (harm if leaked, and to whom) first; only then decide where it goes. Ryan's paper starts with the on-site vs cloud split — we propose reversing the order.
② 逐类追问：企业合约云AI真的是泄露风险吗？ 企业级合约（不用于训练、有保密条款）下的云AI，对大多数类别未必构成实际泄露。只有在合约/法律明确禁止第三方处理，或数据本身是凭证时，才必须排除云端。 For each category ask: is an enterprise-contract cloud AI actually a leak risk? For most categories it may not be. Cloud must be excluded only where a contract/law bars third-party processing, or the data is a credential.
③ 更大的真实风险：内部过度曝光 对KPR多数数据而言，更现实的风险可能是"内部看错人"——通过Bot让不该看的员工或群组（跨部门、跨公司、跨工厂）看到成本、价格、薪资等。权限设计比服务器位置更关键。 The bigger real risk for most KPR data is likely internal over-exposure — the wrong staff or chat group (across departments / entities / factories) seeing cost, prices or payroll through bots. Access design matters more than server location.

3. 待Boss决定的问题 Open questions for Boss

  1. 每份文件由谁决定属于哪一类？（文件所有人 / 部门主管 / IT）默认分类是什么？Who decides the category of each document (owner / dept head / IT)? What is the default when unclassified?
  2. 供应商价格是否允许采购人员通过Bot查看？范围到哪一层？Should supplier prices be visible to purchasing staff via bots? To what level?
  3. 是否安排对现有NDA及客户/供应商协议（如 Sussex NDA）中关于AI/第三方处理的条款做合约审查？由谁负责？Run a contract review of NDA and customer/supplier agreement clauses on AI / third-party processing (e.g. Sussex NDA)? Who owns it?
  4. 跨客户价格（CF / HH / Dunia 等）在群组层面如何隔离？How to segregate cross-customer pricing at the chat-group level?
  5. 多实体（中国工厂含JL、印度ALLIED、印尼MSL、新加坡CS公司、加拿大King Power Safety）之间的数据可见范围如何设定？What data may each entity (China factories incl. JL, India ALLIED, MSL Indonesia, Singapore CS company, Canada King Power Safety) see of the others?
  6. (g) 战略/财务类是否单独成类？Confirm whether (g) strategic/financial is a separate category.
  7. 完成分类后，再评估是否真的需要本地AI服务器，以及放哪些类别。Only after classification: decide whether an on-site AI server is actually needed, and for which categories.
